Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026  ·  LexSec Advisory LLC

1. Who We Are

LexSec Advisory LLC ("LexSec", "we", "us") is a Texas limited liability company that operates the GRC platform at app.lexsecadvisory.com and the marketing website at lexsecadvisory.com. Our contact address is info@lexsecadvisory.com. This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

The Service is intended for users in the United States. If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, please be aware that the Service is not directed to you and we do not represent that the Service complies with EEA or UK data protection law. If you nonetheless choose to use the Service from those jurisdictions, you do so at your own risk and subject to the legal bases described in Section 3 below, which are provided for transparency only.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data — name, work email address, and organisation name when you register.
  • GRC content — risk records, evidence files, compliance notes, and other data you enter into the platform. This data belongs to you.
  • Waitlist data — name, email, and organisation if you join the VeriLink waitlist.
  • Usage logs — server-side request logs (IP address, timestamp, endpoint) retained for up to 30 days for security monitoring.
  • Session cookie — a single HTTP-only cookie used to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies to the processing of your personal data, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the platform services you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest — security monitoring, fraud prevention, and service improvement.
  • Consent — waitlist sign-up and marketing communications (you may withdraw at any time).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To store and retrieve the GRC data you create.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, invitations).
  • To notify you about product updates if you have opted in.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the sub-processors needed to deliver the Service. A current list of our sub-processors, including their roles and data processing locations, is maintained at lexsecadvisory.com/sub-processors and is updated whenever sub-processors are added or removed. As of the effective date of this Policy, our sub-processors include providers of cloud hosting and edge infrastructure, transactional email delivery, and internal team communications.

6. Data Retention

Account and GRC data is retained for as long as your account is active. On account closure, data is deleted within 30 days unless legal obligations require longer retention, or unless retention is reasonably necessary for the investigation, defence, or resolution of a pending or reasonably anticipated dispute, legal claim, regulatory inquiry, or audit obligation. Waitlist entries are retained until you request removal.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, email info@lexsecadvisory.com. EEA/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority, though we note that the Service is not directed to EEA or UK users as described in Section 1.

8. Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA)

If you are a Texas resident, you have the following rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to access — you may request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct — you may request correction of any inaccurate personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to delete — you may request that we delete personal data we have collected about you, subject to applicable exceptions (for example, data we are required to retain by law or that is necessary to complete a transaction or fulfil a contractual obligation).
  • Right to data portability — you may request a copy of your personal data in a portable, readily usable format.
  • Right to opt out of targeted advertising and sale — we do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising, so no opt-out is currently required. If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

To exercise any of the above rights, email info@lexsecadvisory.com with the subject line "Texas Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days of receiving a verified request, as required by the TDPSA. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response and we will review the denial within 60 days. If you are unsatisfied with the outcome of an appeal, you may contact the Texas Attorney General.

For purposes of the TDPSA, LexSec Advisory LLC acts as a "controller" with respect to personal data it collects directly, and as a "processor" with respect to GRC content you enter into the platform, which remains your data under Section 2 above.

LexSec Advisory LLC provides the rights described in this Section as a matter of policy, regardless of whether it is currently legally required to do so under the TDPSA. We believe Texas residents deserve transparency and control over their personal data irrespective of our compliance obligations at any given time.

9. California Residents

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) applies to for-profit businesses that meet specific thresholds, including annual gross revenue exceeding $26,625,000, processing the personal information of 100,000 or more California consumers or households annually, or deriving 50% or more of annual revenue from selling or sharing personal information. LexSec Advisory LLC does not currently meet any of these thresholds and is not legally required to comply with the CCPA/CPRA at this time.

Nonetheless, as a matter of policy and in keeping with our commitment to privacy regardless of legal obligation, we extend the following rights to California residents on a voluntary basis: the right to know what personal information we have collected about you; the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable exceptions; the right to correct inaccurate personal information; and the right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and no opt-out is required. To exercise any of these rights, email info@lexsecadvisory.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days. If our practices or business size change such that the CCPA/CPRA becomes legally applicable, this Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you by email of material changes at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
